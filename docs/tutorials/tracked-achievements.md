# Tracked Page (Achievements)

The Tracked page basically has a special list of achievements you decided to track (by pressing **X**, or the equivalent button for your controller, on it) from the main achievements list on the main page for the current game.

Once an achievement is added here, you can view more info with **A**, including the comments for it. Pressing **X** will untrack it. Finally, pressing **Y** on it will allow you to add a note to it, which you can view right on the achievement in Tracked, as well as define a "tag" for it. A tag is basically a word to categorize it. For example, if you give an achievement a "Side-Quest" tag, that achievement will be organized into that category, which you can collapse or expand. These categories are organized in alphabetical order.

Once an achievement is unlocked, it is automatically removed from your tracked list. There are also a few other useful things here to enhance tracking achievements, such as the ability to add all missables, sorting achievements, bulk tagging achievements, and more.

## Add All Missables

Pressing this button will simply add all of the missable achievements to your tracked list in an instant.

## Apply Tag

You can bulk add the last tag you have assigned to achievements. Let's say you applied the "Side-Quests" tag to your last achievement. You will now see above your tracked list the **L1** button glyph with "Side-Quests" next to it. This means you can now press **L1** on any tracked item to mark it to be tagged.

Once you bulk mark the ones you wish to move to the "Side-Quests" category, to commit it or apply it all, scroll to the top of your list and select the **Apply** button, which looks like a tag. Then all of your tracked achievements that you marked will be changed to that category.

## Clear

This is where you can clear all of your tracked achievements for the current game in bulk. It requires a double press, as this is a destructive process.

## Sort

You can toggle between the sort methods for tracked achievements: **Up Next**, **Manual**, **Most Points**, **Fewest Points**, **Rarest**, and **Most Common**.

When Manual sort is on and you press **R1** over a tracked achievement, it allows you to move it around in any order you like within the category (tag) it is organized in. Simply press **R1** over it and you will see a green cursor around the achievement, move it up or down with the D-Pad, and then press **R1** again when you want to finish sorting. The good news is, it remembers your manually sorted list, so feel free to switch between other sorting methods without worry of losing your custom list.
