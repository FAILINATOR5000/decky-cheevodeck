# Data Transfer Page

This page offers the ability to transfer your data to another device for tracked achievements for the current game, notes for the current game, and milestones. Simply toggle what type of data you wish to transfer, and then toggle whether or not to replace the data completely or not. If **Replace Destination** is toggled on, your data on the current device will be wiped and replaced with the data from the sending device. If off (default) your data is only merged, meaning only new items are sent over and existing items will not be overwritten. Milestones is the exception though. In this case **Replace Destination** is forced to on and the sending device's milestones will be wiped once the transfer to the destination device is complete.

## How it Works

Simply connect both devices to the network and open up the same page. On the device you wish to import the data from your other device, select the other device from the list and the transfer process will begin.
