# Unlock History Page

The Unlock History page is simply a page that shows the history of unlocks for either the current game (if accessed from the main page under the current game) or your friend's current game (if accessed from their game on their [profile](profile.md)). Pressing **A** on the achievement will open the [Achievement Overview](overview-achievement.md) page showing additional info, as well as comments.
