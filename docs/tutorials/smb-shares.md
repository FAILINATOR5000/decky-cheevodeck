# SMB Shares Page

This page makes it much easier for users to mount network storage. Whether you want to mount a NAS to access some ROMs or you want to mount one to set up Kodi to access your movies, this tool has your back! Simply select the **Add Mount** button to get started and you will be walked through the process of setting it up.

Once a mount is established, it becomes independent of CheevoDeck, meaning if you uninstall CheevoDeck, your mount will be untouched. You can, however, choose through SMB Shares to remove the mount itself (not your files, just the mount). Also, if you ever do reinstall CheevoDeck again, it will detect and find the mount you made and show it back in the list again, no problem!

One more thing! If you want to add a convenient folder to your desktop with links to your mounts, select the **Links to Desktop** button. Remember, these are merely links and it's safe to delete them, as they are only references to your mount, which is also a reference to your files as well.
