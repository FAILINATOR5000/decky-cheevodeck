# Social Hub Page: Activity (Feed)

The Activity feed in the Social Hub page is a place where you can view your friends' unlock activity. It shows the latest 500 activities from your friends, chosen by the background service which fetches their activity. Favoriting a friend will increase how often they're fetched each tick.

- Pressing **A** (or whatever the button equivalent is for your controller) on an activity card will by default open the [Achievement Overview](overview-achievement.md) page, where you can view more information about the achievement, as well as comments.
- Pressing **X** on it will bring you to the [Game Info](game-info.md) page where you can view more information about that game, as well as comments.
- Pressing **Y** on the activity card will bring you to the [profile](profile.md) of the friend who unlocked it, where you can view their stats, wall, and much more.

## What's the difference between the Activity feed and the unlocks in the Notifications?

The difference is the unlocks featured in Notifications are one featured unlock each tick, while the Activity feed is all unlocks pulled during that tick, so it's more of a complete list.
