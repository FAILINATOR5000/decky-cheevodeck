# Options Page

The Options page contains over 200 options for those who love customization, but also has a variety of presets for those who prefer to keep things simple. Each option has well-documented help text below it, so be sure to read carefully before changing an option. Chances are there is an option for almost anything you can think of, so feel free to browse the five different categories: System, Display & Notifications, Social, Cache & Data, and Advanced.

## System

The options here are system-related options, such as account, language, services, controller mappings, and more. One notable thing here is the **Settings Profile Chooser**. If you ever want to change between different settings profile presets again, this is the place.

At the very bottom is the **Show Developer Options** toggle. Be careful when toggling this on, as more advanced options will now be visible, mostly under the Advanced tab. A lot of these developer options were for debugging purposes, and can cause strange behaviors if modified, so carefully read over the help text for any of these.

## Display & Notifications

Here you can customize display-related and notification settings. You will find things here like the Display Scaling Presets, quick menu customization, appearance-related settings, and more. You can also turn on and off notifications and toasts for a variety of the services here.

## Social

Everything social is located here, such as friends service settings, activity feed settings, comments service settings, and more.

## Cache & Data

Here, you have complete control over your cache settings as well as your data. For example, you can clear entire lists here in bulk, such as clearing all of your tracked achievements. Because it is a destructive process, some options will require a double or even a triple press to confirm.

You can also clear cache here as well. Luckily, the services will automatically prune and manage your data and cache so it stays in check, but if you ever want it cleared, you can do so here.

## Advanced

In Advanced, you will find more complex options, such as changing the number of worker threads or the comments check frequency for the comments service. Carefully read the help text here before making the decision to change these. Luckily, these are fine-tuned for whatever settings profile you are using.
