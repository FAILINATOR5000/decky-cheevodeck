# Guides Page

Guides is where you can browse and view GameFAQs guides for the current game that you are playing. It shows guides in the order of the latest ones you have used, with the green dot indicating your current guide. You are able to view the guide in the Quick Access Menu (side window), but if you want to view it enlarged, press **A** while on the guide to open it in the enlarged view.

Once you establish a current guide by going into it, you can then view it on every page if you enable **Pin Latest Guide** on the Guides page. Once enabled, there will be a Guides pin on the top of every page so you can access your current guide.

Even better, you can set it up to open your current guide by pressing a button combo or one of the back buttons. While this works by default in the Quick Access Menu (side window), you can expand this to work even when it is closed and you are playing your game by toggling on **Global Buttons** in the Quick Menu. By default, it is mapped to **R5** and the **Menu + D-Pad Down** combo for those who don't have back buttons. You can customize the combo in **Quick Menu > [Options](options.md) > System Tab > Mapped Shortcuts**.

There are two types of guides to be aware of: formatted and unformatted.

## Formatted Guides

These are special guides that are organized into "pages" and may contain special formatting and media. In the guide viewer, you will move from page to page with the **L1** and **R1** buttons.

## Unformatted Guides

This type of guide is the traditional GameFAQs guide, which is one giant chunk of text, typically with a lot of ASCII art. Instead, you will be scrolling all the way down the whole guide as you progress, but the good news is your location is remembered, so you won't lose progress leaving and coming back to it.

## Bookmarking

You are able to bookmark your location in your guide with **Y**. After pressing **Y**, selecting to bookmark it, and giving it a name, you can always access it any time by pressing **Y** again and selecting it from the list. At the top of the bookmark dialog are also some useful buttons that let you jump throughout the guide, such as jumping to the beginning, center, or end.

## Searching

Searching for keywords in a guide is useful if you want to find a certain section within the guide. Searching is only available in the enlarged guide viewer.

When within a guide, simply press **A** to open the search field, then **A** again to open the onscreen keyboard. Type what you want to search and press **B** to exit the onscreen keyboard. Use the arrows on the right to seek throughout the guide for all occurrences of what you searched. Press **B** again to exit search mode.

## Zoom

Press **L2** and **R2** to zoom out and zoom in to whatever is comfortable to you. This zoom setting will be remembered, so you don't have to keep adjusting it each time.
