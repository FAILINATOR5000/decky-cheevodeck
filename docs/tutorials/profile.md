# Profile Page

The Profile page is the central hub where you can view stats, activity, online status, rich presence, currently playing, and much more for either your own profile, a friend's, or a stranger's, depending on how you've accessed it.

At the top, you will see the user's avatar, username, online status, rich presence info, as well as stats and quote if they have one. Under all of that, you will see a row of buttons where you can get even more info about the user: **[Awards](awards.md)**, **[Want to Play](want-to-play.md)**, **External Profile**, **Ranking** (amongst friends; self only), and **[Activity](activity-calendar.md)**.

And below those is the **Games** button, where you can view all of the user's games and see their stats on them. Under the Games button is the **Recent Games** button, where if you expand it, you can see their recently played games, with the current one showing the one they were playing most recently. Selecting either a game in their games list or a game in their recent games places that game card on top of the recent games list and loads achievement info below it in the achievements tab. By default, when you enter the profile, the recent game reflects their true current game.

If you select the second tab to the right of the achievements tab, you will be able to see the wall posts for the current profile and post.

Finally, under the current game info, you will find two or three buttons: **[Unlocks](unlock-history.md)**, which shows the unlock history for the game, as well as **[Leaderboards](leaderboards-list.md)**, which shows the leaderboards for the game. The third one only appears on a friend's or stranger's profile, and that is the **[Compare](compare-achievements.md)** button. This allows you to compare your stats against theirs for their current game.
