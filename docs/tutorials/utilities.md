# Utilities Page

The Utilities page contains a variety of tools that can make achievement hunting easier, whether directly or indirectly.

## [Dolphin Mapper](dolphin-mapper.md)

Tired of mapping controls for all of the different controller types in Dolphin? Never fear, as you can easily map controls with a press of a button from one of the presets or even make your own mapping.

## [SMB Shares](smb-shares.md)

Need to access your games from a NAS media server? Easily add and manage mounts here.

## [Cheevo Check](cheevo-check.md)

Wondering which of your games actually support RetroAchievements? Find out here. The core scan also allows you to add badges to your game pages. Optionally, you can verify the dumps in your ROM library to determine if your file is corrupted or a bad dump.

## [File Watcher](file-watcher.md)

Paranoid about your files decaying from bit rot or a failed controller like I am? Set up a schedule and monitor their integrity here. Manual scans supported too. Monitoring persists across system restarts and will resume where you left off.

## [Stormbreaker](stormbreaker.md)

Protects you from a rare, long-standing SteamOS glitch where the Quick Access Menu (QAM) gets stuck and your controls stop responding. Stormbreaker watches for the focus race that causes it and breaks it up the moment it starts, so the menu just blinks. If a freeze ever gets past it, Automatic Recovery restarts Steam's interface while your game keeps running, so you can select Resume and jump right back in.

## [Transfer Memories](transfer-memories.md)

Tool to import or export your saved memories from/to other devices.

## Calculator

The ultimate tool for all serious achievement hunters... a calculator.

## Web Browser

A custom-built browser made to integrate with CheevoDeck. Great for viewing other types of guides like spreadsheets, Smogon, etc. Uses the SteamOS browser at its core, but with added enhancements, such as tabs, bookmarks, ad-blocking, downloads, and better media compatibility.
