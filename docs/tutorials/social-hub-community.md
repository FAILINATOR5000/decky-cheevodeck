# Social Hub Page: Community

Within the Community section of Social Hub, you will find the **Subscribed** and **Saved Comments** tabs, where you can keep track of your subscribed discussions as well as comments you have saved from various discussions.

## Subscribed

In every comments section you visit within CheevoDeck, you will find a **Subscribe** button. Once subscribed, they will appear here, and you will also have notifications sent to the Bell icon, with possible toasts notifying you when someone posts to it.

When pressing **A** (or whatever the equivalent is for your controller) on a subscribed game, achievement, or event discussion, you will be brought to the overview page for it with the comments section open for your viewing.

To unsubscribe, you can either select the **Unsubscribe** button in the associated section or select the trashcan button on the item in your subscribed list. Up to 10 items maximum can be subscribed to.

## Saved Comments

Whenever you see a comment in any comments section, if you really like the comment, or if it has useful links or info and you want to save it offline, select it with **A** or open it in the viewer, then select the star in the upper right corner to save it. It will then appear in your Saved Comments list.

To remove the saved comment, you can either unstar it in the viewer or use the trashcan button in the list.
