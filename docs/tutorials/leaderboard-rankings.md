# Leaderboard Page (Rankings)

This shows the results for the currently selected leaderboard. If you have partaken in it, your results and standing are shown at the top, letting you know. You are also within the ranked list, assuming you are at or under rank 500.

You are also able to filter the leaderboard rankings to just you and your friends by toggling the filter button to **Friends**. Select it again to toggle it back to **All**.
