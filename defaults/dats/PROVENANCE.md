## What I changed

The DAT files are not shipped as they came. Each one was parsed and re-emitted
as gzipped JSON, and two fields were dropped:

* **md5 and sha1 removed.** CRC32 is what a zip's central directory already
  hands us for free, and identity plus integrity is the whole claim being made.
  Dropping the other two is most of the size saving.
* **Everything else per entry removed** — region, serial, release dates and the
  rest. What remains is the game name, the rom name where it differs, and one
  `[size, crc]` pair per track.

Share-alike applies to this directory. It does not reach the plugin code, which
is a separate work shipped alongside rather than an adaptation of the database.
See `LICENSE` at the repository root.
