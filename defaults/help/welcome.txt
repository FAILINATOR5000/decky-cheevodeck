Hello, I hope you are doing well! I just want to give you a warm welcome, along with some quick advice that'll get you started in no time. Here are a few good pointers:

1. CheevoDeck loads your achievements for you. All you have to do is start an RA-supported game in your favorite emulator while signed into it with your RetroAchievements account. Then open the side window <b>Quick Access Menu</b> with the <b>...</b> button or open CheevoDeck, and you are good to go! Everything is loaded, and you are ready to hunt achievements.

2. Make use of the <b>Quick Menu</b>! On the <b>Main Menu</b> in the upper-right corner, press the button that looks like a hamburger to access it. From here, you will find shortcuts to different parts of CheevoDeck, along with some useful toggles.

3. When no longer playing RetroAchievements games, on the main page, select the <b>Quick Menu</b> and toggle the <b>Do Not Disturb</b> button to on. That way, you won't get any popup toasts for achievement unlocks while playing non-RetroAchievements games.

4. Your achievement progress can show up right on the game pages in your Steam library, on anything you've added as a non-Steam shortcut with Steam ROM Manager. If you would like this feature, you can enable it by going to the <b>Quick Menu</b> on main and then by selecting <b>Cheevo Check</b>. Scroll down and turn on <b>Steam Library Badge</b>. It works off a Cheevo Check scan to identify your ROMs with great accuracy, so for this to work its magic, you first have to scan your entire ROM library here as well. So be sure to select the <b>Scan</b> button. Once it is started, it can take a while with very large libraries, but no worries! You can leave Cheevo Check while it is running and do whatever you like. It will let you know when it is done scanning. Once finished, you will start to see your achievement progress on your favorite games, and you can go back to the <b>Cheevo Check</b> page to see which scanned files are supported by RetroAchievements.

5. Take advantage of button shortcuts! By default, you can press <b>Start</b> to view your notifications on any page. Also, you can press <b>Select</b> which will warp you to the top of any page in an instant. You can assign more actions to other buttons in <b>Quick Menu > Options > System tab > Mapped Shortcuts</b>.

6. Use the <b>B</b> button to go back to the previous page you were just on. If used on the <b>Main Menu</b>, you will exit the CheevoDeck plugin. Also pressing it while in the <b>Quick Menu</b> will conveniently close it.

7. Take advantage of the tons of options. If there ever has been a time you thought, "I wonder if I can turn this off?" or "Can I change this?", the good news is you most likely can. The <b>Options</b> page, accessed from the <b>Quick Menu</b>, contains over 200 options to fine-tune. Not into fine-tuning? There are a lot of presets to make things easier for you. Check them all out!

8. For the friends list in the <b>Social Hub</b> if your friend's avatar seems off, press <b>Y</b> to <b>Resolve</b> and fix the wrong avatar image. The roster service is very smart and is able to solve 99% of the issues with wrong avatar images, but there is the super rare less than 1% chance where a friend's avatar does not match. This is due to how RA stores image names on their media server. This app takes an educated guess as to what the matching avatar is for a friend given their username without having to hit the RA API with too many requests. In a rare situation where a user renames their username, leaving behind the old avatar under the old username and then your friend creates their username as that user's old username, this might yield an inaccurate image. Yeah, sounds confusing, because it is! Resolve simply asks the server where the image is, which can be expensive; hence why I make it a manual button vs having it automated by the service. Just being respectful to the RA servers!

9. Got some serious goals to make? Try <b>Mastery Goals</b>. It lets you create a goal that can be named anything. For example, "Zelda Games". Then you can add each Zelda game to it, and you can track the series as a whole. It's... a set for sets, yeah! But seriously, try it out. It's a very powerful tool to set goals. Want to beat every Zelda game? Every JRPG? Every Barbie game? <b>Mastery Goals</b> has you covered!

10. The community is the backbone! We can all agree, the best part of RetroAchievements is the friendly community. If you have any questions, don't be afraid to ask! CheevoDeck makes it very easy to post comments on games, achievements, and walls. If you need help, go to the comments section in the app for a given game or achievement. Select <b>Post</b> and you will be forwarded straight to the comments section. You will have to enter your RA password for the first time, but once set up, you are good. Then after posting your comment, you can even subscribe within CheevoDeck to that comment. You will be sent a notification straight to your <b>Notifications</b> feed when someone replies as well as sent a popup toast you can even see while playing your game. Also, if you ever see a useful comment with valuable information for the game or achievement you are working on, you can save that comment, which can be viewed in the saved comments section in the <b>Social Hub</b>. To save any comment or wall post, just press <b>A</b> on the comment to open it in the extended view dialog. In this dialog, press either <b>X</b> or select the star button to favorite it.

With these tips in mind, you will become a CheevoDeck pro in no time! This will keep it forever so you are able to view this message for future reference in the <b>Archived</b> tab in <b>Notifications</b>.
