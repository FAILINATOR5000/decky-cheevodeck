CheevoDeck {{VERSION}}

This update is quite a hefty one, with one highlight being the new updater feature. You can now choose to install an updater to be placed on your Desktop upon first-time onboarding or by manually pressing the <b>Add Updater to Desktop</b> button in the About page. This adds another option alongside downloading the ZIP or copying the URL to install. All it takes is a simple double click of the launcher in Desktop mode, and everything is updated.

The next major change is the addition of arcade games support to Cheevo Check, as well as Steam library badges that, when enabled, show a badge on your library game pages with your achievement progress. You can interact with it to be brought straight to a detailed info page for the game. Because it relies on Cheevo Check to obtain hashes from your game library to determine if supported on RetroAchievements, you must run a Cheevo Check scan of your whole library. In EmuDeck, that would be your /home/deck/Emulation/roms folder, which the scan option opens up to by default. Once your files are hashed and verified, each of those games will display a badge. If you add any new games to your library, run the scan again; luckily it becomes faster as it caches your local hashes by default.

<b>New</b>

- The CheevoDeck Updater. A button in About installs it to your desktop, and there's a toggle for it during first-time setup.

- RA badges on your Steam library pages for non-Steam games, showing achievement progress. Select one to jump to Game Info.

- Arcade support in Cheevo Check, which is what makes arcade badges possible.

- Developer messages. If a SteamOS or client update breaks something, I can push a message straight to your notifications with a workaround until there's a proper fix.

- A welcome message after first-time setup, covering the basics of getting around.

- View Style joins the first-time setup dialogs, so you can pick Default or Compact alongside the social and scaling presets.

- Darken Screen on Cheevo Check and File Watcher, so an OLED panel can rest through a long scan.

- Visit RA is now available both as a mapped shortcut and as a Quick Menu button.

- A scroll indicator in the text viewer, and its default zoom is down to 130.

<b>Fixed</b>

- A long username with no spaces in it pushed the title strip off to the right on Friend Profile, Want to Play and All Games.

- Finishing or cancelling a Cheevo Check scan left the page with no focus at all.

- A factory reset made the What's New card announce a release you'd been running for weeks.

- An update found while you were still entering your credentials lost its notification, because there was no account for it to live in yet. It waits for one now.

- An archived What's New card showed the notes for whatever version you had installed instead of the release it was announcing.

- The About page gave the new version number twice, once in a smaller font under Check for Updates.

- The up-to-date message mentioned a force option that isn't there.

- Truncated comment cards were missing their A prompt.

- L1 and R1 made no sound when cycling a mapped shortcut, though A did.

- The save and add buttons in the credential modals moved to the left, to match everything else.

- The This Game tab is now Current Game.
