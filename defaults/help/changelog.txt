This one is a follow-up to the quality of life improvements in 0.9.0 and aims to iron out those pesky bugs! Both Tracked and Notes pages had some strange behavior going on, but no worries, the infrastructure has improved drastically. I also added some very minor quality of life improvements as well, such as changing the formatting of Tracked and Game Notes categories. Now, by default, it no longer is in all caps and now matches whatever case you type. If you liked the old style, you are able to change it back in options. See below for more!

<b>Tracked Page</b>

-Fixed the cursor vanishing after editing a note.
-Changing an item's tag now leaves the cursor in the category you were already in, instead of following the item over to its new one. If you're tagging a lot of items in one go, this saves you walking back to your place every time.
-Fixed the page shifting under you while you scroll up through categories.
-Notes are no longer cut off at 300 characters. The editor always let you type 500, so now it reflects that.
-The Completed tag no longer shows up in your tag suggestions.

<b>Game Notes Page</b>

-The same cursor and page-shifting fixes as Tracked above.
-Changing a note's tag also leaves the cursor where you were instead of following the note.

<b>Options</b>

-Added Formatting settings for header case. <b>Tracked Header Style</b> and <b>Notes Header Style</b> control your tag categories on those two pages, where Typed shows the tag exactly as you typed it. Capitalized shows the Steam default capitalized headers. Also, <b>General Header Style</b> covers every other header. View them in <b>Quick Menu</b> > <b>Options</b> > <b>Display & Notifications</b> tab under <b>Formatting</b>.

<b>Miscellaneous Fixes</b>

-Fixed categories coming back collapsed when they should have been open. It only sorted itself out if you closed and reopened the panel, and it could happen on Tracked, Other Games, Game Notes and Dolphin Mapper.

Alright, I hope this helps improve your achievement hunting journey even more! I'm working on a very special new feature that will most likely be included in the next update. See you around!
