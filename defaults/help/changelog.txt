This is just a smaller update with some fixes and improvements for the Play Together feature. Most notably, your friends list now syncs across devices.

In case you've missed it, here are the 2.0 notes:

Welcome to the Play Together update! (Plus MUCH more). You are now able to play your favorite games with your friends easily with the new Play Together feature, making retro game multiplayer easier than ever, unified all under this one feature. No matter where you and your friends are in the world, you are now able to host rooms, invite them and/or open it so anyone else can join. Once in the room, you can chat, voice chat (if enabled), chill in the lounge, and play your favorite games together. Itching to trade Pokémon or battle it out in Super Smash Bros.? Play Together has you covered! To get started, select the notification bell in the upper right corner to see the banner. This is just one feature, so please do read below to learn more about Play Together and the other features, bug fixes, and improvements.

<b>New: Play Together</b>

-Host or join rooms (public, private, or friend-only) with up to 9 other players, locally, online, or via Offline Hotspot. The host picks the game and play mode, then everyone readies up, and the host then launches the game, which automatically starts for everyone.

-Add friends to your Play Together friends list by their current RetroAchievements username. You will also be given friend suggestions too if both you and a mutual friend on RA are using the service. Friends can invite each other directly to rooms. Your friends' status shows if they are online or not and what game they are currently playing. You are able to hide your status under the Friends tab.

-In a room, there are three types of play modes the host can choose from: Link Play, Couch Play, and Stream Play.

-Link Play simulates connecting two devices together, like linking up two Game Boys like the good ol' days. This is excellent for games like Pokémon and Advance Wars, for example.

-Couch Play simulates playing classic couch games like Mario Party, Secret of Mana, Super Smash Bros., etc. Back then, you would have to play together all on one TV. Now with this feature, it utilizes RetroArch's (and other standalone emulators') netplay, where everyone can play a game together remotely.

-Finally, there is Stream Play, where only the host needs a copy of the game and streams the game to all of the guests, while the guests stream their inputs to the host and everyone plays together. Stream Play is powered by Sunshine on the host and Moonlight for the guests, both of which CheevoDeck sets up for you. Hosting Stream Play needs AMD graphics, which every Steam Deck and the Steam Machine have, but anyone can join as a guest.

-To compare the play modes side by side, select the description under the play mode you picked and press A to View More.

-The comparison chart is also in the Play Together tutorial, under the ? button on the Play Together page.

-Play Tic-tac-toe, Connect Four, Reversi, and Checkers in the room's lounge.

<b>New: Offline Hotspot</b>

-Are you camping or have dodgy Wi-Fi? You are now able to directly connect Steam Decks (or other SteamOS devices) together wirelessly without any Wi-Fi to play your games.

<b>New: Play/Host/Play With.../Save Backups/Dolphin Mappings Button</b>

-Under each game there is now a Play button with a chevron (more on that later), where you can launch your games directly. To identify where your game files are, it's recommended to run a Cheevo Check scan on your entire game library. Pressing A on the Play button when it is grayed out will assist you with setup to get it working. In addition, you are able to quickly host your game from here (Host), select emulator overrides per game (Play With...), and view and restore game save backups (Save Backups) by selecting the chevron next to the Play button and selecting which per-game feature you want to explore. I almost forgot, if it's a Wii or GameCube game, you can set per-game Dolphin mappings as well.

<b>New: Fix It Feature</b>

-Whenever a game needs setup, by either selecting a grayed-out button or "Requires Setup" next to the Play Together play mode selection, this feature is a one-click feature that gets you set up in no time. Whether you are missing an emulator or need to install a tool, Fix It has you covered, so there is no hassle. It tells you exactly what it will do for you, and you select the "Fix It" button.

<b>New: Save Backups And Versioning</b>

-By default, your cartridge games are backed up at regular intervals, at launch, and at the end of the session. This can be disabled per game or by system. If you ever mess up or the emulator corrupts your game, you are able to restore the save effortlessly.

<b>New: Manage Emulators (Utilities)</b>

-Easily view your current emulators, add more, and update existing ones.

<b>New: Data Transfer (Utilities)</b>

-Move tracked, notes, guides, events, milestones, or everything between your SteamOS devices.

<b>New: Activity Stats Table</b>

-Days active, longest streak, points, best day, and top game.

<b>New Milestones (In-app achievements)</b>

-Added tons of new milestones for the new features.

<b>New: My Games</b>

-The game search box that you access via the Quick Menu now has two tabs: My Games and All Games. All Games behaves how it did before, where you can browse all games that are on RA. My Games is more personalized, where it merges your local games found on your device via a Cheevo Check scan as well as what games you have progress in via RA.

<b>Quality of Life / Improvements</b>

-<b>The QAM opens faster and smoother:</b> long lists draw one screen first, hidden pages stop redrawing, and the notification dots no longer pulse forever (that pulse cost about a third of a CPU core).

-<b>File picker:</b> a new <b>Mounts/SD</b> list for SD cards, USB drives and network shares. A share that's offline shows as <b>Not Reachable</b>.

-<b>Web Browser:</b> a Legacy Sites whitelist for old sites with broken HTTPS (like the classic starmen.net), and YouTube ad blocking on live streams.

-<b>Darken Screen</b> can be mapped to a button and used as a Global Button.

-<b>Comment threads</b> stay on the card you were reading across a QAM close.

-<b>Dolphin Mapper:</b> Reorder Controllers, PowerA wired Xbox controller support, and smoother long lists. Also added additional mappings for Dolphin Stream Play. Controllers from connected clients send their abstracted controls as an Xbox controller, so the mapping reflects this.

-<b>Mastery Goals:</b> a Keep Games Page Fresh option.

-<b>Memories:</b> a controls hint on the clip share sheet, a wider share sheet for screenshots, and the full IP in the Memories Sent notification.

<b>Quality of Life: Game Pickers</b>

-Picking "All systems" remembers the system, and a system with no achievement games says so.

<b>Heads Up</b>

-A lot of what's new runs off two things: a Cheevo Check scan of your retro game library, so CheevoDeck knows where your game files are, and your games or emulators added to Steam, so it can launch them. That covers the Play button, Play Together rooms, Save Backups, Play With… and per-game Dolphin mappings, and the scan is also what fills the My Games tab in the improved search. If you added your games to Steam with Steam ROM Manager (EmuDeck does this for you), you're already set, and if not, just having your emulators in Steam is enough, which the Fix It feature, as mentioned above, can do for you.
