This is a smaller update compared to the last, but it aims at better accessibility and assistance at the first screen. It also focuses a little bit on Dolphin Mapper.

<b>What's New</b>

- You can pick your language right on the setup screen now before you enter anything. No more having to go to options to change it after the intro.

- A <b>Getting Started</b> button was added to the startup page which opens a guided walkthrough.

- Dolphin Mapper supports the Xbox 360 wireless controller and its dongle. Fourteen starter mappings have been added.

- A <b>Help</b> button was added to the Dolphin Mapper page leading to a small tutorial.

<b>What's Fixed</b>

- Fixed a problem where the language picked during setup was wiped during the onboarding process. It also survives a settings reset now.

- Dolphin Mapper tags are sorted alphabetically now.

- The save button in the mapping form moved to the left for convenience.
